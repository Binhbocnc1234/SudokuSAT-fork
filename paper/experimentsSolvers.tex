Table~\ref{tab:solvers} compares MiniSat 2.2~\cite{minisat}, Glucose 3.0 and 4.1~\cite{glucose}, CaDiCaL 1.9.5~\cite{cadical} and Kissat 4.0.4~\cite{kissat}. Up to $36\times36$ the five solvers stay within 0.9~s of each other, because Python encoding takes most of the time. CaDiCaL and Kissat are slightly slower on small grids for a technical reason: PySAT cannot interrupt them, so they run in a separate process that is killed at the limit, and starting it costs about 5 to 10~ms. At $49\times49$, the formula has 11.3 million clauses and takes a median 3.7~s to encode, and the solvers separate: MiniSat, CaDiCaL and Kissat solve all 5 puzzles in a median 10 to 12~s, while both Glucose versions solve only 2.
